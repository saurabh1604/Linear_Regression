Why not always use OLS?

The OLS solution requires inverting a matrix: \( \beta = (X^T X)^{-1} X^T y \). Matrix inversion has a complexity of roughly \( O(n^3) \). For massive datasets (millions of rows/features), this becomes too slow.

3. Machine Learning Perspective (Gradient Descent)

In Machine Learning, we treat this as an optimization problem. We want to descend the "Cost Surface" to find the minimum error.
